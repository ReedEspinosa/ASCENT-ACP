- PSD TRUNCATION vs INSTRUMENT SIZE RANGES (2026-08-31, investigate
  before rerun; measured with the table engine on real V4 PSDs):
  * 2021 submicron: neph is PM1 (impactor; cut diameter CONVENTION
    UNSTATED in ICARTT headers, PM1 usually = 1.0 um AERODYNAMIC =
    ~0.77 um geometric at rho~1.7) but our psd_max_um=1.0 cuts at bin
    CENTER dp<=1.0 um (LAS dry AmmSO4-optical diameter, top bin edge
    1.06 um). The 0.77-1.06 um band carries median 14.7% / p90 33% of
    modeled Sc550 -> if the impactor is aerodynamic-1.0, we
    systematically over-include scattering vs what the neph saw (RRI
    biased low). RESOLVED 2026-08-31: impactor is D50 = 1.0 um
    AERODYNAMIC per Schlosser et al. (2025, ISARA paper — now cited in
    the nc references attr). psd_max_um set to 0.75 (= 1.0/sqrt(1.77),
    AmmSO4 density consistent with LAS sizing) for the 2021 submicron
    variant in configs + PsdConfig default; deliberately kept a plain
    config field for easy adjustment in later reruns. 2021 grid is now
    48 bins (SMPS 30 + LAS 18, top center 0.708 um, edge ~0.75).
  * BOTH years: PSAP absorption is Bulk/Total (no impactor) while the
    2021 PSD is submicron -> supermicron absorption inflates IRI.
    Same inconsistency exists in LARGE's own SSA product (documented
    in their header); document ours in the nc metadata at minimum.
  * 2020 total: grid ends at LAS 3.16 um centers (3.55 edge) vs inlet
    ~5 um; neph also under-registers coarse scattering (angular
    truncation). Bins 2.0-3.55 um already carry median 8% / p90 38% of
    modeled Sc550 -> total-variant CRI is coarse-tail sensitive; the
    3.55-5 um unmodeled band adds a high-RRI bias of unknown size.
  * LAS sizes are dry OPTICAL diameters calibrated for AmmSO4 (RI 1.52);
    retrieved RRI != 1.52 makes the size grid slightly inconsistent with
    the retrieval (heritage ISARA limitation; note in metadata).
- V5 CHI2 DIAGNOSIS (2026-08-31, after V5 shipped): the 2021-vs-2020
  min-chi2 gap (retrieved windows: 0.185 vs 0.065; success rate 57% vs
  91%) is NOT absorption (abs carries only 4-10% of chi2) — it is
  scattering SPECTRAL SHAPE. 2021 model under-predicts sca at all
  wavelengths, worst at 700 nm (median -22%), modeled AE too steep by
  +0.29; 14% of windows push RRI against the 1.56 ceiling (p90=1.545).
  Matched-window 700nm residual: V4 sharp-1.0um cut -9.2% -> V5
  sharp-0.75um cut -20.4%, i.e. BOTH sharp cuts under-predict red
  because a D50 impactor TRANSMITS ~50% at its cut and rolls off
  gradually — the measured submicron sca genuinely contains partial
  0.75-1.5um contribution that a step cut at any diameter removes
  entirely. Secondary effect: min-chi2 ~3.5x higher at Sc550<20 Mm-1
  (20% relative sigma understates the absolute noise floor in clean
  marine air). PROPOSED FIX (V6): replace the sharp cut with the
  impactor penetration curve — weight LAS bins by T(D_aero) with
  D50=1.0um aero (Schlosser et al. 2025) and a configurable sharpness
  (typical impactor GSD ~1.1-1.2; ask LARGE for their curve), using the
  same rho=1.77 aero->geo mapping. Config: impactor_d50_aero_um +
  impactor_sharpness alongside psd_max_um. Expected: red residual
  healed, RRI un-railed, 2021 success rate up. 2020's smaller opposite
  signature (model AE too FLAT by -0.16, -5.6% at 450) is consistent
  with uncorrected neph angular truncation of the coarse mode.
  -> V6 RESULTS (2026-08-31): 2020 bit-identical (impactor disabled ✓).
  2021: modest gains only — CRI ok 2595->2738, kappa 2528->2669, 700nm
  residual -21.8->-20.4%, dAE +0.29->+0.25, RRI railing 14->9%. Follow-up
  experiment (60 windows, reported CRI held fixed): NO impactor steepness
  closes the red deficit — broader tails (gsd 1.35/1.6) make it WORSE
  (the log-logistic loses more below D50 than it gains above for our
  steeply falling PSDs). A uniform diameter scale of ~+4% (well inside
  the LAS +/-10% correlated sizing uncertainty, Moore et al. 2021)
  closes BOTH channels' magnitude and most of the slope: D*1.10 swings
  450/700 residuals to +28/+23%. CONCLUSION: the remaining 2021 misfit
  is degenerate with LAS sizing scale, not an impactor-shape problem.
  Implications: (a) keep gsd=1.15 pending LARGE's measured curve; (b)
  the D-scale nuisance will dominate the V7 uncertainty budget; (c) a
  log-D fudge factor was considered and REJECTED by Reed (2026-08-31)
  in favor of the raw-signal refit below.
- HIGH PRIORITY — NOW SCOPED (2026-08-31, see LAS_RI_CORRECTION_PLAN.md):
  LAS RI sizing correction = the raw-signal refit realized as per-
  candidate bin-diameter remapping (bins are signal thresholds, so the
  two are equivalent). Feasibility VERIFIED: the MOPSMAP sphere files
  carry Mishchenko ALPH1 Legendre coefficients of P11 (reconstruction
  normalizes to 1.0000), so a LAS-geometry partial-scattering response
  kernel qsca_partial(mr, mi, x) can be added to the table extract (v2)
  offline. Open before implementation: exact LAS 3340 collection
  geometry/azimuth (nominal 22-158 deg at 633 nm — confirm vs Moore et
  al. 2021 / TSI), the multivalued-region smoothing convention matching
  instrument firmware, residual lnD prior, and whether to build the
  UHSAS 1054 nm kernel in the same pass. Validation gates culminate in
  the SEAC4RS rerun: sizing_lnD_shift +0.181 -> ~0 and the PI-Neph RI
  comparison regaining correlation.
  Original idea (superseded framing): fit the LAS RAW OPTICAL SIGNAL
  instead of the AS-calibrated PSD. The
  2021 misfit looks like particle RI differing from the AmmSO4
  calibration; rather than patching the calibrated diameters, invert the
  calibration: map each LAS bin back to the raw scattering-signal
  amplitude the instrument actually recorded (via the AS response
  curve), and inside the retrieval forward-model that signal for each
  candidate CRI (scattering cross-section integrated over the LAS
  collection geometry and 633 nm phase function) — sizing then becomes
  self-consistent with the retrieved RI and the Moore et al. (2021)
  circularity disappears. Known catches to scope before committing:
  (1) the 633 nm Mie response is NON-MONOTONIC above ~0.5 um (UM 3a) —
  the signal->size mapping is multi-valued exactly where we need it
  most, so the forward model must work in signal space (predict counts
  per signal bin), never invert; (2) needs the LAS collection-geometry
  kernel (solid angles, Mie phase-function integral) — get from TSI
  docs/PI or fit to the published AS calibration; (3) absorbing
  particles depress the response (IRI enters sizing); (4) SMPS bins are
  mobility-based (RI-independent) — only the LAS part transforms, the
  stitch point needs care; (5) kappa/wet states grow the PSD in
  GEOMETRIC diameter, so the signal-space fit must still carry a
  geometric representation internally.
  -> IMPLEMENTED in V6 (2026-08-31): log-logistic penetration weighting
  P(D_a)=1/(1+(D_a/D50)^s), D50=1.0um aero, 16-84% gsd=1.15 (assumed;
  ask LARGE for their curve), rho=1.77; applied to the RETRIEVAL PSD
  only in isara_bridge (reported dndlogdp unweighted); curve exported as

8. Full optical set (CRI, sca, abs, ext) x 3 RH states x wavelength dim
   ANSWER (how ISARA fits): single spectrally-FLAT (RRI, IRI) pair; grid
   search RRI 1.51-1.54 (0.01 step) x IRI 0-0.030; forward-models ALL six
   channels (sca 450/550/700, abs 470/532/660) with that one CRI; candidate
   accepted only if all 3 sca within 20% AND all 3 abs within 1 Mm^-1;
   final = mean of accepted candidates, re-verified. So all wavelengths are
   in the fit criteria, but there is no spectral RI freedom — reporting CRI
   on the wavelength dim = same value repeated (document flatness).
   - TODO [HIGH IMPORTANCE]: per-wavelength CRI retrieval (pair adjacent
     sca/abs channels 450/470, 550/532, 700/660 -> 2 obs per band, 2
     unknowns). The flat-RI assumption drives systematic error in the
     retrieved spectral properties AND systematically prevents retrieval
     (all-channel 20% criterion unmatchable) exactly in the
     high-spectral-dependence cases where it matters most. Caveats to
     design around: exactly-determined per band (noise maps 1:1 into CRI);
     needs Angstrom interpolation between sca and abs wavelengths. Not in
     this round, but next in line after V4.
   - CRI grid findings: the 1.51-1.54 grid is DRY-ONLY. Wet/ambient RI is
     derived by volume mixing with water (RRI_w=(RRI_d+(gf^3-1)*1.33)/gf^3)
     and floats freely below 1.51 (-> 1.33 as gf grows), so no wet-side
     restriction. DECIDED: expand dry grid to ~1.47-1.56. Costs (all
     acceptable): LUT rebuild (~2.5x candidates, one-time, cache
     auto-invalidates via fingerprint); mean-of-accepted estimator can
     shift even for windows that retrieved fine before (new edge candidates
     pass the 20%/1 Mm^-1 tests and pull the mean) -> do the grid change as
     its own final step and quantify the before/after shift; more windows
     will retrieve successfully (previously edge-clipped). Consider also
     reporting n_accepted / std of accepted candidates as an uncertainty
     proxy.
   - Wet/ambient CRI: derive by kappa volume-mixing with water (1.33+0i),
     gf at 80% and at RH_amb_used. Also spectrally flat. Report it.
   - Humidified sca/ext/SSA at all wavelengths: extend ISARA wet forward
     calc to full wavelength list (same MOPSMAP call, cheap); run it at both
     80% and ambient RH. Humidified absorption = ext - sca; document that it
     is purely model-derived (absorption only measured dry).
   - NOTE: narrow RRI grid (1.51-1.54) bounds everything downstream; worth a
     mention in file metadata.

9. PSD at 3 humidity states [decided: shift + SURFACE-conserving remap +
   spillover variable]
   gf is size-independent (bulk kappa) -> pure uniform shift in logD:
   logDp_wet = logDp_dry + log(gf).
   Plan: convert to dS/dlogDp (surface), shift bin EDGES by gf, overlap-remap

   remapped dS/dlogDp back to dN/dlogDp via target-bin dp_mid^2.

   parameter range. Number and volume then NOT exactly conserved under

   - SPILLOVER [decided: option (b)]: KEEP the original bin grid so

     (Relevant: at RH=96%, kappa=p95=0.18 -> gf ~ 1.75.)
   - Windows without kappa -> wet/ambient PSD = fill.
